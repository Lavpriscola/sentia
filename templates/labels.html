Purpose of Sentiment Labels

These sentiment labels serve as training signals for machine learning models to:

  • Detect Emotional Tone: Identify positive enthusiasm, nostalgia, anger, disappointment, sarcasm, or neutrality
  • Flag Risk Indicators: Pinpoint malicious content, coordinated attacks, or unreliable information
  • Assess Bias: Distinguish between objective reporting and emotionally charged commentary
  • Predict Impact: Estimate potential for virality, controversy, or influence on streaming numbers

Classification Guidelines
Best Practices:
  • Consider the overall tone and intent of the message
  • Look for emotional indicators and context clues
  • Pay attention to sarcasm and implicit meanings
  • Consider the potential impact on the K-Pop community
  • When in doubt, choose the most conservative classification
Common Challenges:
  • Mixed Emotions: Use "Mixed/Conflicted" when multiple sentiments are present
  • Sarcasm Detection: Look for quotation marks, emojis, and context
  • Cultural Context: Consider K-Pop specific terminology and references
  • Coordinated Attacks: Look for patterns and organized language
